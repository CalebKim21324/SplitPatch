Item blast radius

One item change reaches every champion who buys it, and how much depends on when they buy it. Each row puts the item in the slot that champion actually buys it in (lolalytics slot tables, with the average minute it's finished), simulates the change, and weights by purchase share. The verifier's ripple guard allows bystanders to move at most ±2%.
