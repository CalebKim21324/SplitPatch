Every patch, the whole roster.
Only verified changes ship.

SplitPatch reads solo queue stats for every champion, plus what players say on Reddit. It picks who needs a buff or a nerf, has an AI agent propose a small number change, checks that change in a deterministic simulator it cannot edit, and writes patch notes and tooltips that a second checker matches against the diff.

Backtest against Riot’s real patches

For six champions over patches 26.16 to 26.21, each system saw only that patch’s data and made a call. We scored it against what Riot changed in the next patch. Patches 26.19 to 26.21 were held out while we tuned.

The rule baseline scores well on agreement because Riot holds most champions most patches, and the rule almost always says hold. It never caught a real change.

Call by call

Top: SplitPatch’s call. Bottom: what Riot did next patch. Outlined: they match.

Six of SplitPatch’s twelve false alarms are Garen nerfs, raised when the strong line was a flat 52.5%. Garen is banned a lot, so the ban-rate line now clears him in 26.22. The backtest hasn’t been rerun with that line, because it needs every champion’s ban rate for each old patch.
